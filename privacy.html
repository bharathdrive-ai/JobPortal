Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026

TalentHub is an internal careers portal, currently a prototype. It helps employees find and apply for jobs inside the organisation and helps recruiters review applications. This policy explains what personal data TalentHub handles, why, and how it is protected.

Who is responsible

TalentHub is operated by the organisation's HR team (the "administrator"). Questions or requests about your data go to the administrator, using the support email shown on Google's consent screen when TalentHub is connected to Google, or through your HR contact.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, work email, user ID, employee ID, department, grade, current designation, months with the company and years of relevant experience. Passwords are stored only as salted one-way hashes.
  • Applications: the jobs you apply for, your optional cover note, your resume, and the status of each application.
  • Assessment records: recruiters' scores, supporting evidence quoted from your resume, comments and decisions.
  • Technical data: a sign-in session cookie, and settings saved in your browser such as your light or dark theme.

We do not collect or use age, gender, religion, ethnicity, nationality, marital status, disability, photographs or other sensitive personal characteristics, and these are never used to score candidates.

How we use it

  • To let you sign in, apply for jobs and track your applications.
  • To let authorised recruiters, hiring managers and administrators review applications and record decisions.
  • To keep the service secure, for example by limiting repeated failed sign-in attempts.

Assessment scores support people's decisions; they never make hiring decisions on their own. We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or use it to train artificial-intelligence models.

Google user data

TalentHub connects to the administrator's Google account to store resumes in a private Google Drive folder and to keep the application register in a Google Sheet. It requests only the drive.file permission ("see, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app"). This means TalentHub can only access the folder and files it creates itself; it cannot see any other files in that Google Drive.

  • Files are used only to store and show resumes and application records inside TalentHub.
  • Files are never made public or shared with anyone outside the people authorised in TalentHub.
  • Google data is not transferred to third parties, used for advertising, or read by people except authorised HR users doing their recruitment work, or as needed for security or to comply with the law.

TalentHub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who can see your data

  • You can see your own applications. You can't see other candidates' data or internal assessment scores.
  • Recruiters and hiring managers can see applications for the roles they manage.
  • Administrators can manage accounts and see all applications.
  • Service providers: the site is hosted by Vercel, and resumes and records are stored in Google Drive and Google Sheets. They process data only to provide those services.

How long we keep it

Applications and resumes are kept while needed for the recruitment process and then deleted in line with the organisation's HR retention policy. You can ask for your data to be deleted sooner, subject to legal obligations.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS. Sign-in is checked on the server and sessions use a signed cookie that page scripts can't read. Access to applications depends on your role. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

Your choices and rights

  • Ask the administrator for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it.
  • Withdraw an application or replace your resume from My Applications while it is still open.
  • The administrator can disconnect TalentHub from Google at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Cookies and browser storage

TalentHub uses one essential cookie to keep you signed in, and your browser's storage for preferences such as the theme. It does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
